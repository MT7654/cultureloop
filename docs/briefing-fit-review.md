# CultureLoop briefing fit review

11 October 2026. Assessment from Malcolm's six supplied briefing screenshots, the local submission template and the implemented application. This is an advisory assessment, not an organiser eligibility ruling or a predicted judging score. No application changes made in this review.

## Recommendation

Keep CultureLoop. Narrow the demonstration and cultural argument; do not rebuild the concept. Proposed main-track pair: Design × Tech and Culture × Tech. Design × Tech currently has the clearest evidence through functional spatial interaction, original taste-derived sound and the transition between memory, interests and next experiences. Culture × Tech remains credible but needs a more explicit cultural interpretation than credit-aware event recommendations.

Malcolm confirms multiple tracks may be selected. The distributed submission template says up to TWO of FOUR options: Design × Tech, Culture × Tech, Best Use of Tech, Common Ground Award. The slides describe the latter two as cross-track awards but do not establish whether entry is automatic. Check the live form/organiser clarification before final selection. No additional track has been confirmed or submitted.

## Award priorities

- Best Use of Tech: credible Codex centrality (60%) from the actual research/design/code/audio/3D/QA process. Inventiveness (40%) needs concrete before/after evidence, showing how Codex helped turn cultural relationships into functional interaction. More dependencies or an unused database do not strengthen this argument. There is no runtime AI recommendation model to claim.
- Common Ground Award: weakest fit to the described rubric because 60% concerns visible different backgrounds/skills on the team and 40% concerns their perspectives. Malcolm is the sole human participant; AI agents are tools, not invented teammates. This is a strategic assessment, not a solo-entry eligibility prohibition.
- People's Choice: use the constellation, a clear unexpected connection and audible user-initiated music as the live hook. No award outcome is predicted.

## Proposed bounded plan

1. Position CultureLoop as an interactive map of connections between personal taste and Singapore cultural experiences. Keep the Culture Pass balance as practical context.
2. Curate the demonstration to three existing sourced chapters: one familiar, one bridge and one open exploration. Keep the seven-event catalogue underneath. Demonstrate one actual bridge, such as film/storytelling into audience-directed topical theatre, using the existing source-supported explanation and programme note.
3. If approved for the next refinement, sharpen one existing preview's cultural note: what changes across these artistic forms, the actual programme feature behind the bridge and the user's reason to explore. Keep sound explicitly original interpretation; do not call generic genre synthesis an authentic cultural recording.
4. Freeze new backend/auth/database integration, catalogue expansion, runtime AI and additional screens. Preserve working 3D/audio/list/mobile/accessibility behaviour.
5. Rehearse an adjustable two-minute demo: personal memory and remaining credits → tastes → select a bridge in 3D → explain its cultural meaning → play the original mix → save the next chapter. Confirm the actual presentation time with the organiser if needed; two minutes is a suggestion, not a supplied rule.
6. Complete judge-accessible demo/source links, track-specific reflections, factual AI attribution and a short fallback recording. Record Malcolm's actual contributions and learnings in his own words. Check the final form's two-option handling before choosing the final track/award pair.

These are proposed scope/presentation refinements, not newly implemented features or confirmed track selections. Pending shared Page updates remain local because no Pages capability is exposed.
