# CultureLoop research and assumptions

Checked 11 October 2026. This prototype tests a **continuity hypothesis**: some people may need a personally meaningful reason to return after their first cultural outing. We have not established prevalence, causes, increased redemption, repeat attendance or conversion. The S$55 outing / S$45 remaining story is an illustrative demo persona. No linked Culture Pass account is used.

## Source evidence

Seven real future or ongoing eligible experiences are curated from official SG Culture Pass and authorised ticketing pages. Source URLs, verified date, verification method and known price caveats are stored with each event and documented in [data-provenance.md](data-provenance.md). Most content was retrieved from search-indexed primary pages because direct SG Culture Pass opens returned HTTP 403; indexed crawls ranged from today to three weeks earlier. BookMyShow's Fat Kids page opened in full. This is snapshot verification, not live inventory. A Writers Festival booking product was excluded because its retrieved primary page said sold out.

Prices represent the listed starting base ticket or named standard advance tier. They can change, exclude fees and may carry package restrictions. Musica Intimae's official portal lists from S$50 while its festival organiser lists S$55; the interface price note preserves that discrepancy. Affordable Art Fair's S$24 standard advance tier ends 22 November; concession and child prices are not used as a standard adult estimate. Users must confirm price, admission rules, dates, eligibility and availability through the source before booking.

No event photographs, footage or audio were licensed or copied. Abstract artwork and Web Audio synthesis in the implementation are creative interpretations and must remain labelled as such.

## Product interpretations

Interest tags, suggested visiting contexts and cultural bridges are editorial judgements grounded in described artistic forms. They are not organiser endorsements or validated psychological predictions. A deterministic local score combines taste overlap, past affinity, context, base-ticket budget, authored bridges and date relevance, with category diversity. Scores must never appear as scientific match percentages. Workshop and film interests can lead to an adjacent visual-art or performance experience; the small catalogue does not contain every chosen category.

Balance is entered manually. Saving, viewing and opening an official link do not spend credits or verify attendance. No demographic inference, authentication, payment processing or analytics are required. Weekday-evening/weekend preference is informational where complete session schedules are absent; reliable date ranges can support overlap filtering without inventing sessions.

## Validation still needed

Browser and automated tests establish technical behaviour only. Participant testing, accessibility evaluation by disabled users, and longitudinal cultural-engagement outcomes remain unmeasured. A useful next study is a small consenting walkthrough asking whether the explanation makes an unfamiliar experience appealing and trustworthy; record actual answers before claiming findings. Refresh catalogue sources before a public or later-date demonstration.
